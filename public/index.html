Android & iPhone download help

Allow Node.js through Windows Firewall on private networks only. Guest Wi-Fi, VPNs, and client isolation may block transfers.

Android: scan the QR code with your camera or Google Lens, open the link in Chrome, and tap Download PNG. In Tesla, open Creations → Wrap → Upload and select the PNG from Downloads. If the picker only shows photos, use its Browse or Files option if available, or open the PNG in Files and save/copy it to Pictures so it appears in your photo picker.

iPhone: save to Files, or press and hold the wrap image to save to Photos. Then select the PNG in Tesla Upload.

Direct file sharing may be unavailable on local HTTP. Download PNG works without it. Sending the PNG as an email attachment also works on either platform.
